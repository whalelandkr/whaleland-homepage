POLICYWHAT WE DO AND DON'T HOLD

  1. What we collect on this site

    NOTHING

    This site has no sign-up, no login, no cart, no payment and no forms. We do not ask for your name, contact details or payment information anywhere on it, and we hold no database of visitors.

  2. What your browser stores

    When you first arrive, the site checks your browser's language setting once and sends you to the matching language folder. To avoid doing that again on every visit, it saves a single flag in your browser's local storage:

    • cov_lang — value 1. Nothing else. It records only that the check has already happened.

    This flag stays in your browser and is never sent to us. Clearing your browser storage removes it, and the site keeps working either way. We use no advertising or tracking cookies.

  3. Hosting and server logs

    The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, that service records requests it receives, which can include IP addresses. Those logs belong to the hosting provider and are handled under its own policy — we neither receive them nor keep copies.

  4. Fonts loaded from other services

    Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts and jsDelivr. Your browser contacts those services directly to fetch the font files, which means they see the request. We don't receive anything from that exchange.

  5. When you buy something

    Purchases happen on our online shop, not here. Whatever you enter there — name, address, contact details, payment — is collected and handled under that shop's own privacy notice, which you should read before ordering. This page does not cover it.

  6. Things we haven't switched on yet

    We intend to add a contact form, a newsletter sign-up and website analytics. None of these are running today. When any of them goes live, we will update this page first — with what is collected, why, and how long it is kept — and the change will be dated.

  7. Sharing and outsourcing

    NONE

    Because we hold no personal information from this site, there is nothing to pass to third parties, nothing entrusted to a processor on our behalf, and nothing transferred abroad.

  8. Your rights

    Under Korean law you may ask to see the personal information an organisation holds about you, have it corrected or deleted, or ask that its processing be stopped. Since we hold none from this site, there would be nothing to show you — but if you want to check, write to us and we will confirm.

    For anything you entered while buying, please direct the request to the shop where you placed the order.

  9. Changes to this notice

    If what we collect changes, or the law requires it, we will revise this notice and post it here with a new effective date. Material changes will be posted before they take effect.
