PACKING LISTOPERATION 01 — SEOUL

  • 01Classified envelope× 1

    Kraft paper, closed with a string tie, the agency crest printed front and back under a TOP SECRET seal. Opening it is the first part of the briefing, so take your time with it.

  • 02Agent passport× 1

    Forty pages at 88 × 125 mm, bound in a leathertex laminate cover with a matte gold foil crest. Fifteen districts, thirty places, forty-five assignments — and thirty illustrations of Bummy in disguise, one for every place you're sent.

  • 03Stamp stickers× 20

    Clear stickers carrying the agency emblem, cut to sit inside the seal ring on each district page. Fifteen for the districts and five spares, each with a line for the date and time you finished.

  • 04Boarding pass card× 1

    Shaped like a real airline ticket. Write in your agent name, agent number and arrival date, and it becomes a pass that exists exactly once.

  • 05Agent ID set× 1 set

    Bummy's own PVC identification card, with a card holder and a lanyard to wear it on. It's built to the detail of a real staff pass, right down to Bummy's signature — and it arrives finished, so there's nothing to fill in.

  • 06Bummy keyring× 1, random

    Fifteen designs, one for each district, and Bummy is dressed for every one of them. Which one lands in your envelope is a surprise.

ALSO INCLUDED A QR code on the last page opens an after-action report, where you can send your results back to headquarters once the trip is over. It's entirely optional — the fifteen districts work start to finish on paper alone, with nothing to install and no venue to check in with.
