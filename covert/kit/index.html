Six physical items sit inside one document envelope.

PACKING LISTOPERATION 01 — SEOUL

  • 01Classified envelope× 1

    A kraft document envelope that closes with a string. A TOP SECRET stamp is printed on the front.

  • 02Passport mission book× 1

    The emblem is stamped in matte gold foil on a leather-textured laminated cover. Zones, places and assignments run in order.

  • 03Seal stickers× 20

    Clear stickers that let the printing underneath show through. They are cut to the seal space on the page, with the emblem inside the circle.

  • 04Boarding-pass mission card× 1

    A card shaped like an air ticket. You fill in the agent name, the agent number and the immigration field yourself.

  • 05Agent ID set× 1 SET

    A PVC agent ID comes with a card case and a lanyard. Both sides arrive printed, so there is nothing to fill in.

  • 06BUMMY keyring× 1

    One of fifteen kinds is included at random. The kind cannot be chosen.

Also included The QR inside the passport lets you send a report to headquarters. It is optional — all fifteen zones run to the end on paper alone, with no app to install and no partner venue to visit.

AFTER THE TRIPAFTER YOU GET HOME

The back of the passport holds pages meant to be filled in after the trip is over.

What stayed as an object and what stayed with you are written apart, and there is a place to paste one photograph.
